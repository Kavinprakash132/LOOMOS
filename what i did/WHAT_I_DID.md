## 0. Quick 30-Second Summary (How to Explain LOOMOS)

> **LOOMOS** is a mobile-first production management, analytics, and smartphone cloth-defect inspection web app built for small decentralized powerloom job-work units in Tamil Nadu.  
> Instead of paper notebooks, a supervisor or owner opens LOOMOS in Chrome over local shed Wi-Fi on an Android phone or laptop (`Choice 1`), logs daily shift metres in `<90 sec`, manages warp beams & weft wastage with live Beam Finish ETA badges, calculates 4-mode weaver/helper wages with trilingual (`EN / தமிழ் / हिन्दी`) printable payslips, catches trader per-metre rate cuts, runs on-device CPU ONNX + Grad-CAM cloth defect inspection (`/inspect`), forecasts weekly output & bi-monthly EB bills, downloads formatted 6-sheet Excel (`.xlsx`) & print-PDF statements, and simulates 20–100 loom expansions (`/analytics`).  
> Everything uses **₹0 free & open-source tools** (React + TypeScript + Tailwind + FastAPI + SQLite + PyTorch/ONNX + `openpyxl`), stores money in integer paise, never hardcodes business rules, and records every financial edit/soft-delete in an immutable audit log.
