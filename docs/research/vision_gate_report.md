## 2. Experimental Protocol (Strict Zero-Leakage Strip Split)

- **Split rule:** All `104` valid defect strips and `141` clean strips were split **at the strip level** (`70%` train / `15%` validation / `15%` test) so patches from the same cloth strip never appear in both training and test sets.
- **Test set (`604`–`620` non-overlapping `256×256` patches):** Heavily imbalanced (~`4.5%` defect prevalence: `27–28` positive patches vs `577–592` clean patches), mirroring real floor inspection where most cloth is clean.
- **Hardware:** Local laptop CPU (`PyTorch 2.12.1+cpu`, 10 threads, zero GPU required).

## 4. Grad-CAM Visual Inspection Findings (Verified Held-Out Test Split Only)

Direct visual review of 4 panels generated by `MobileNetV3-Small` on the strictly **held-out test split (`16` test strips, `0` strip overlap with the `72` training strips)**:
- **Broken End (`code 002`, prob `1.00`):** Grad-CAM cleanly highlighted the vertical warp break down the cloth.
- **Fuzzyball (`code 019`, prob `0.94`):** Grad-CAM ignored the bright white selvedge edge on the left and focused directly on the small defect spot.
- **Broken Yarn (`code 006`, prob `1.00`):** Grad-CAM tracked the vertical broken yarn streak closely.
- **Tiny Warp Ball (`code 025`, prob `0.49`):** A ~5×5 px speck against a coarse checkered weave was missed (`0.49` < `0.50`) and Grad-CAM drifted to the top border—demonstrating why `256×256` single-crop recall on sub-10 px specks is the main bottleneck and why overlapping/multi-crop phone inference + `LOOMOS-Shed` fine-tuning (`RQ3`) is scientifically justified.

## 5. Gate Conclusion
- **Decision:** **PASS.** `ResNet-18 + CLAHE` reaches **0.950 ROC-AUC** and **96.69% accuracy** at **37 ms/image on CPU** with **99.0% specificity** (`6` false alarms out of `577` clean patches), and `MobileNetV3-Small` runs in **6.5 ms/image**—both fast enough for local FastAPI/ONNX CPU serving with zero GPU cost.
- **Next ML actions (Week 3–5):** Add `EfficientNet-B0`, test overlapping tiles / lower threshold (`0.20–0.30`) to boost defect recall on tiny specks, evaluate `TILDA_400` + `FabricSpotDefect` for multi-class pretraining, and execute `RQ3` cross-domain experiments on `LOOMOS-Shed` smartphone photos.
